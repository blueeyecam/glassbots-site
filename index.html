Where we don't fit

We'd rather tell you now:

  • Real-time, sub-second decisions. Fraud scoring, ad bidding, high-frequency anything. Our architecture optimises for legibility, not latency.
  • Heavily regulated sectors. Health, financial services, government. These need specialist compliance partners; we'll say so and point you at one where we can.
  • If nobody on your team can open a text file. You don't need a developer — but you need one person willing to read.
  • If your system is actually fine. Roughly one in five we assess is healthier than its owner fears. The Diagnostic will tell you that too.
For Australian businesses: from 10 December 2026, amendments to the Privacy Act require businesses subject to it to be able to explain decisions their automated systems make about people. Most systems will answer that with an explainability layer added after the fact. Ours answer it structurally: every decision step is a readable file with its evidence attached — because it was never hidden in the first place.
